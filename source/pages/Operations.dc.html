How is this different from the Fractional CFO service?

Fractional CFO covers finance leadership: reporting, cash, the board and fundraising. Operations covers how revenue, billing, systems and compliance actually run day to day. Many clients use both.

Which systems do you work with?

Salesforce, QuickBooks, Sage, Xero and Excel among others. We're system-agnostic: the aim is the right fit for your size and stage.

Do you work with international businesses?

Yes. The team has run operations across multiple countries, and works in English and Spanish.
